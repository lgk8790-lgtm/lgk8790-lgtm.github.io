    01 · THE NAPKIN

    I wanted to build something of my own.

    Bangalore, October 2024. Two years into AkzoNobel, I kept sketching the same idea: live events that college crowds actually show up for. I called it GigVig.

    02 · THE WIREFRAME

    Then I drew the skeleton.

    Events, tickets, artists, venues. Before a single booking, I mapped how a night out should work, from the first post a student sees to the moment they're through the door.

    03 · THE TEAM

    Then I brought in people.

    A team of 8 across marketing, ops and logistics. I negotiated 10+ partnerships with artists, venues and sponsors, and cut costs 20% by optimizing the contracts.

    04 · THE LAUNCH

    Then the lights came on.

    Six large-scale events across college and off-campus venues, delivered end to end on tight timelines.

    05 · THE TRACTION

    Then people came.

    2,000+ attendees in three months. I tracked every campaign with Excel and marketing analytics, doubled down on what worked, and lifted ticket sales 30%.

    06 · THE PIVOT

    Then I knew what I was missing.

    Running GigVig showed me exactly where I needed sharper tools: technology strategy, product and scale. So I took the next pivot, to NYU.

    07 · THE NEXT NAPKIN

    Then a new idea started popping.

    At NYU, the founder itch came back, and this time I had the tools. Poping is a nightlife and event-ticketing platform for Gen Z in New York, taking the GigVig playbook to a much bigger market.

    08 · THE WIREFRAME

    Back to the skeleton.

    Same playbook as GigVig, on a smaller screen. Before drawing a single button, I studied the US ticketing market from first principles, including how Ticketmaster came to dominate it.

    09 · THE BUILD

    Then it started to pop.

    That research set Poping's positioning, product scope and go-to-market: who it's for, what to build first, and how to reach them.

    10 · NOW

    Still building.

    I co-founded Poping in May 2026, and it's what I'm building right now in New York. Into nightlife, ticketing or Gen Z? Let's talk.
